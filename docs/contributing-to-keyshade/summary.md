We are an [open-source](https://github.com/keyshade-xyz/keyshade) organization. We rely on contributions from developers in the community, and we are thankful for them. Even the smallest contributions, such as changing the name of a variable or fixing typos, are very much appreciated.&#x20;

This series of documents helps you set up and develop Keyshade locally on your device. Start anywhere you feel like!
